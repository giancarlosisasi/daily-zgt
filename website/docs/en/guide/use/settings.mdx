# Settings

## Open the settings

Click the gear key in the footer of the panel. The settings view replaces the content of the panel. The footer stays.

To close the settings, do one of these:

- Click the gear key again.
- Click the back button **‹ Ajustes** at the top.
- Click a tab, **Prioridades** or **Notas**.

## The four rows

The view has four rows, in this order. The text of each row is in Spanish, as in the app.

| Row | Text under the title | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `Abrir al iniciar sesión` | `Abre Daily ZGT cuando inicias sesión en tu Mac.` | off |
| `Máximo 5 prioridades abiertas en Hoy` | `Con 5 abiertas en Hoy, no puedes añadir ni mover otra a Hoy.` | off |
| `Siguiente prioridad en la barra de menús` | `Muestra junto al ícono el título de la primera prioridad abierta de Hoy.` | on |
| `Atajo global` | `Abre la ventana de captura desde cualquier app.` | `⌃⌥⌘P` |

The first three rows have a switch. The fourth row has a field for the shortcut.

### Abrir al iniciar sesión

The switch makes the app start when you log in to your Mac. It uses the system API `SMAppService.mainApp`, with no helper app.

In some cases macOS needs your approval. The switch then stays on, and the row shows `Falta tu aprobación en Ajustes del Sistema.` with the link `Abrir ajustes`. The link opens the page of login items in System Settings. Approve Daily ZGT there. The row updates when you return to the app.

If macOS rejects the change, the row shows `macOS no aceptó el cambio.`

The app does not store this setting in the database. macOS keeps it.

### Máximo 5 prioridades abiertas en Hoy

When the switch is on and Hoy has 5 open priorities, the app does not add or move another priority to Hoy. It shows `Hoy ya tiene 5 prioridades abiertas.` **Deshacer**, **Regresar** and the daily copy are never blocked. See [Priorities](/guide/use/priorities).

The app stores this setting in the table `app_state`, under the key `today_limit`.

### Siguiente prioridad en la barra de menús

When the switch is on, the menu bar shows the title of the first open priority of Hoy next to the count. The title is cut at 24 characters. When the switch is off, the menu bar shows only the icon and the count.

The app stores this setting in the table `app_state`, under the key `menu_bar_title`.

### Atajo global

The field shows the shortcut that opens the [capture window](/guide/use/capture). The default is `⌃⌥⌘P` (Control, Option, Command and P).

- To change the shortcut, click the field. The field asks for a new shortcut. Press the new keys. The shortcut needs the key Control, Option or Command, or a function key. If macOS or the menu of the app already uses the keys, the field can show an alert.
- To clear the shortcut, click the clear button in the field, or press Delete while the field records. The detail text then says `Sin atajo: la ventana de captura no se abre desde otras apps.`
- To stop the recording with no change, press Escape.

The app stores this setting in the preferences of the app, not in the database. The key is `KeyboardShortcuts_captureWindow` in the domain `com.giancarlosisasi.dailyzgt`. Read it with this command:

```bash
defaults read com.giancarlosisasi.dailyzgt KeyboardShortcuts_captureWindow
```

## Motion

The app has no setting for motion. It follows the macOS setting **Reduce motion**. When that setting is on, the app shows no motion. The motions are: the panel pop, the row that slides out when a priority is done, the caret turn, the detail slide in, the list slide back, and the check mark that draws when a list is empty.

## Where the app stores state

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Open tab, open list, state of **Hechas** | Table `app_state`: `open_tab`, `open_scope`, `done_open` |
| The limit of 5 and the title in the menu bar | Table `app_state`: `today_limit`, `menu_bar_title` |
| Start at login | macOS |
| Global shortcut | Preferences of the app |
