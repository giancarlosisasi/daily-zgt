# Introduction

Daily ZGT is a macOS menu bar app. It keeps the priorities and the notes of the day and the week.

The app lives in the menu bar. It has no Dock icon and no main window. You click the menu bar icon, and a dark panel opens. Next to the icon, the app shows the count of open priorities of Hoy. It can also show the title of the next one.

## Product rules

- The app is dark mode only.
- The text in the app is Spanish.
- The look and the fonts (Gabarito, Onest and Martian Mono) come from the Tecla design system.
- Only the checkbox marks a priority done, and it asks first.
- The app keeps all data in one SQLite file on your Mac, with no account and no server.
- The Claude review is planned for v2. It is not built. It is the only feature that sends data out of your Mac, and only when you click its button.

## What v1 does

- Two lists of priorities: **Hoy** (today) and **Semana** (this week). See [Priorities](/guide/use/priorities).
- Three levels for each priority: high, medium and low.
- An optional due date and time, shown as a chip.
- A chip, `De ayer` or `Desde vie 9`, on an open priority that stays in Hoy after the day changes.
- An optional limit of 5 open priorities in Hoy.
- **Hechas** keeps the priorities done in the last 24 hours. **Historial** keeps the older ones.
- Subtasks and a note for each priority. See [Detail and subtasks](/guide/use/detail-and-subtasks).
- A daily repeat for a priority. See [Daily repeat](/guide/use/repeat).
- One note for each day and one note for each week. See [Notes](/guide/use/notes).
- An alert with sound 30 minutes before the due time and at the due time. See [Alerts](/guide/use/alerts).
- A global shortcut that opens a capture window from any app. See [Capture window](/guide/use/capture).
- A settings view behind the gear key. See [Settings](/guide/use/settings).

## Status of v1

v1 is built. It has no Claude review. That feature is planned for v2. See [Claude review and the API key](/guide/claude/api-key).

## How you get the app

There is no App Store build and no download. You build the app on your own Mac from the source folder. Start with [Requirements](/guide/getting-started/requirements).
