# FAQ

## I do not see the icon

On Windows, click the up arrow next to the clock, **Show hidden icons**. Drag the Daily ZGT icon to the taskbar to keep it in view.

On macOS, a full menu bar can hide icons, also behind the notch. Quit another menu bar app, or turn off the next priority title in [Settings](/guide/features/settings) to make the item shorter.

## Alerts do not show

Check that notifications for Daily ZGT are on, and that Do not disturb (Windows) or Focus (macOS) is off. Then click **Test alert** at the bottom of the panel. See [Alerts](/guide/features/alerts).

## The shortcut does not work

Daily ZGT must run for the shortcut to work. Another app can also use the same keys. Open [Settings](/guide/features/settings), click **Global shortcut** and press other keys.

## Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

The installer has no code signature, so SmartScreen warns you. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. See [Install](/guide/getting-started/download).

## How do I move my data to another computer?

Install Daily ZGT on the new computer. Quit the app on both computers. Then copy the `DailyZGT` folder from the old one to the same place on the new one. See [Privacy and your data](/guide/help/privacy).

## Is it free?

Yes. Daily ZGT is free and open source under the MIT license. There is no account and nothing to pay.
