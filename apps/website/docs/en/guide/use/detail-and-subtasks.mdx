# Detail and subtasks

## Open the detail view

Click the title of a priority, or the meta line under it. The detail view slides in.

A text selection on the title does not open it. If you drag across the title, or the pointer moves more than 3 pt between the press and the release, the detail view stays closed. You can then copy the text of the title.

## What the detail view shows

From top to bottom:

1. A back button, **‹ Hoy** or **‹ Semana**, and a trash button. The back button names the list of the priority.
2. The title.
3. Chips: the state `Pendiente`, the due chip, the chip `De ayer` or `Desde vie 9` (in Hoy only), and the subtask count `N/M subtareas`.
4. A box with five rows.
5. The section **SUBTAREAS**.
6. The section **NOTAS**.
7. The button **Marcar como hecha**.
8. The creation date.

The trash button removes the priority at once. It asks no confirmation.

## The title

The title is a text field. Click it to edit it in place.

- Press Enter to save the title.
- Press Escape to drop your change.
- Click outside the field to save the title.
- The app does not save an empty title or a title that did not change. The field returns to the saved title.
- The app removes spaces at the start and at the end of the title.

## The box

| Row | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Prioridad** | Three keys: **Alta**, **Media**, **Baja**. |
| **Fecha límite** | `Sin fecha` sets a due date and time. The field starts at the next full hour. The x button clears it. |
| **Lista** | Two keys: **Hoy** and **Semana**. The priority moves, and the panel shows the new list. |
| **Repetir** | Two keys: **No** and **Cada día**. See [Daily repeat](/guide/use/repeat). |
| **Alerta** | Text only. It shows `30 min antes y al vencer`, or `Sin fecha, sin alerta` when the priority has no due date. |

If the limit of 5 is on and Hoy is full, the key **Hoy** in **Lista** does not move a priority from Semana. A line shows `Hoy ya tiene 5 prioridades abiertas.` under the row. See [Priorities](/guide/use/priorities).

When macOS alerts are off and the priority has a due date, the row **Alerta** shows a bell with a slash, the line `Las alertas están desactivadas.` and the link `Abrir ajustes`. See [Alerts](/guide/use/alerts).

## Subtasks

Add a subtask in the input `Añadir subtarea y Enter`. Type the text and press Enter. The app removes spaces at the start and at the end, and ignores an empty text.

Subtasks show in two places: under the row, after you click the caret, and in the detail view.

A subtask checkbox marks the subtask done at once. It asks no confirmation. Click it again to open the subtask again. The progress in the meta line of the row, for example `1/3`, counts the done subtasks.

The app has no control to remove or to rename a subtask. A subtask goes only when its priority goes.

When you mark a priority done and it has open subtasks, the confirmation bar says how many are open.

## The note of a priority

The section **NOTAS** has a text box with the placeholder `Contexto, links, lo que falta`.

- The app saves the note while you type, 400 ms after you stop. It also saves when you close the detail view or the panel loses focus.
- If the save fails, the text `No se pudo guardar la nota.` shows.
- A note with only spaces is saved as empty.
- When a priority has a note, its row shows a note icon.

This note is not the same as the notes of the day and the week. See [Notes](/guide/use/notes).

## Mark done and the creation date

The button **Marcar como hecha** asks the same confirmation as the checkbox of the row. After you confirm, the detail view closes and the bar **Deshacer** shows.

The last line shows the creation date: `Creada hoy`, or a date such as `Creada vie 9 oct · 16:00`.

If the app cannot save a change, the text `No se pudo guardar el cambio.` shows.
