# Alerts

A priority with a due time gets two alerts.

- One alert 30 minutes before the due time. Its title is `Vence en 30 min`.
- One alert at the due time. Its title is `Prioridad vencida`.

The text of the alert is the title of the priority. Each alert plays the default system sound. macOS shows the alert when the panel is closed.

The app sets only the alerts that are still in the future. A due time in less than 30 minutes gets only the alert at the due time. A due time in the past gets none.

A priority with no due date gets no alert. A priority that is done gets no alert.

## Permission

The app asks for the notification permission the first time you set a due date. The first press of the bell button also asks, if the app did not ask before. The app asks once. Click **Allow** in the macOS dialog.

If you click **Don't Allow**, macOS shows no alert. The app then shows that alerts are off. To change this later, see [When alerts are off](#alerts-off) and [Troubleshooting](/guide/help/troubleshooting).

## Actions

The alert has two actions.

- **Abrir** opens the panel on the tab **Prioridades**, shows the list of the priority, scrolls to it and highlights it.
- **Posponer 15 min** sets a new alert in 15 minutes. It needs the priority to have a due date.

## Test the alerts

Click the bell button **Probar alerta** in the footer of the panel. The app sends a test alert 5 seconds later. Its title is `Prioridad vencida` and its text is `Revisa tu lista de hoy`. It has the same actions as a real alert.

## When alerts are off \{#alerts-off}

Alerts are off when you did not allow notifications in macOS. The app shows this in three places.

- The bell button in the footer shows a bell with a slash. The help text of the button is `Las alertas están desactivadas.` The button sends no test alert.
- The form **Nueva prioridad** shows a bell with a slash and the line `Las alertas están desactivadas.` in place of the hint, when you set a due date.
- The row **Alerta** of the detail view shows the same line, when the priority has a due date.

Each line has the link `Abrir ajustes`. It opens the notification settings of macOS. Turn on the notifications of Daily ZGT there. The app then shows the normal bell again.

## How the app keeps alerts correct

The app writes each alert in the table `alert`. Each row has a `kind`, a `fire_at` time and a `status`.

| Column | Values |
|---|---|
| `kind` | `before_30m`, `at_due`, `snooze` |
| `status` | `scheduled`, `delivered`, `snoozed`, `cancelled` |

- When you change the due time, the app moves the alert.
- When you mark a priority done or delete it, the app cancels the alert.
- When you click **Posponer 15 min**, the app adds a row with the `kind` `snooze`. The row of the alert that you answered gets the `status` `snoozed`.
- When the app starts, it compares the table with the pending requests of macOS. It fixes any difference.
