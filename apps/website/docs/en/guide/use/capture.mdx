# Capture window

The capture window adds a priority to Hoy from any app. You do not open the panel.

## Open the window

Press the global shortcut. The default is `⌃⌥⌘P` (Control, Option, Command and P). The app must run.

A small window opens near the top of the screen where the pointer is. It floats above other windows, and it shows on full screen apps. The window has:

- A bar with the target mark and the title `Nueva prioridad`.
- One text field with the placeholder `¿Qué es prioritario?`. The field has the focus.
- The hint `Enter agrega. Escape cierra.`

## Add a priority

Type the title and press Enter.

- The app adds the priority to **Hoy**, with the level **Media** and no due date.
- The window closes. The app that you used before gets the focus back.
- An empty title adds nothing.

If the limit of 5 is on and Hoy has 5 open priorities, the window stays open. It shows `Hoy ya tiene 5 prioridades abiertas.` See [Priorities](/guide/use/priorities).

If the app cannot save the priority, the window stays open and shows `No se pudo guardar la prioridad.`

To set a level or a due date, open the priority in the panel after you add it.

## Close the window

- Press Escape.
- Press the shortcut again.
- Click outside the window, or switch to another app.

The window closes with no change. The next time, it opens with an empty field.

## Change or clear the shortcut

Use the row `Atajo global` in the [settings](/guide/use/settings).

- Click the field and press the new keys to change the shortcut.
- Click the clear button, or press Delete while the field records, to clear it. With no shortcut, the window does not open from other apps.

The app stores the shortcut in the preferences of the app, not in the database. The key is `KeyboardShortcuts_captureWindow` in the domain `com.giancarlosisasi.dailyzgt`.
