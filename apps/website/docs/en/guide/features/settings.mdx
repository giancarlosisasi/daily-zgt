# Settings

Click the gear at the bottom of the panel to open the settings.

<img src="/shots/en/settings.png" alt="The Settings view with three switches, Open at login, Maximum 5 open priorities in Today and Next priority in the taskbar, and the Global shortcut field set to Ctrl+Alt+Shift+P." width="360" />

| Setting | What it does | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Open at login** | Opens Daily ZGT when you sign in to your computer. | Off |
| **Maximum 5 open priorities in Today** | With 5 open in **Today**, you cannot add or move another one to **Today**. | Off |
| **Next priority in the taskbar** | Shows the title of the next priority of **Today**. | On |
| **Global shortcut** | The keys that open the [capture window](/guide/features/capture). Click the field and press new keys. | Ctrl+Alt+Shift+P on Windows, Control+Option+Command+P on macOS |

On Windows, the next priority shows when you hover over the tray icon. On macOS, it shows next to the icon in the menu bar.

<img src="/shots/en/tray.png" alt="The tray icon with the number 4, under its tooltip, which reads Daily ZGT · 4 and the next priority, Review the Lima contract." width="240" />

The macOS app shows these settings in Spanish.
