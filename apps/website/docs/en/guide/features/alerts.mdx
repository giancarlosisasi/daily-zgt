# Alerts

Give a priority a due time and Daily ZGT reminds you twice, with sound:

- 30 minutes before the due time.
- At the due time.

<img src="/shots/en/new-priority.png" alt="The New priority form with a due time set. Under it, the hint Alert with sound 30 min before and at the due time." width="360" />

Each alert shows the title of the priority and two buttons. **Open** opens the panel on that priority. **Snooze 15 min** reminds you again in 15 minutes.

## Test alert

Click the bell, **Test alert**, at the bottom of the panel. A test alert comes 5 seconds later.

## When alerts do not show

If notifications for Daily ZGT are off, the bell has a slash and the panel says `Alerts are turned off.` Click **Open settings** and turn on notifications for Daily ZGT.

- **Windows.** The link opens Settings > System > Notifications.
- **macOS.** The app asks for permission the first time you set a due time. Click **Allow**. To change it later, the link opens System Settings > Notifications.
