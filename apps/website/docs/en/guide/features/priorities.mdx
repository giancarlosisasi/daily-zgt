# Priorities

A priority is something you want to get done. The **Priorities** tab holds two lists, **Today** and **Week**. The most important ones show first.

<img src="/shots/en/priorities.png" alt="The Today list with four open priorities. Each row has a checkbox, a title, level bars and a due time chip such as In 1 h 6 min." width="360" />

## Today and Week

Click **Today** or **Week** to switch lists. Keep in **Today** what you do today and in **Week** what can wait a few days.

<img src="/shots/en/week.png" alt="The Week list with three priorities: Write the sprint summary, Plan the quarter and Pay the hosting." width="360" />

A priority you do not finish stays in **Today** the next day, with the chip `From yesterday`.

## Add a priority

Click **New priority** and type a title. Choose a level, **High**, **Medium** or **Low**. Set a due time if it has one. Then press Enter or click **Add**.

<img src="/shots/en/new-priority.png" alt="The New priority form with the title Book the room for Friday, the level High selected, a due time set, and the hint Alert with sound 30 min before and at the due time." width="360" />

The list puts high priorities first, then the ones due soonest. The due time chip turns orange when less than 2 hours are left, for example `In 36 min`, and red when it is `Overdue`.

## Mark it done

Only the checkbox marks a priority done, and it asks first. Click **Done** to confirm.

<img src="/shots/en/confirm.png" alt="The row Send the proposal with the question Mark as done? and the note 2 subtasks are still open, with the buttons Done and Cancel." width="360" />

Changed your mind? Click **Undo** in the next 5 seconds.

<img src="/shots/en/undo.png" alt="A bar that reads Done: Send the proposal, with the button Undo." width="360" />

## Completed and History

**Completed** shows what you finished in the last 24 hours. Click **Reopen** to put one back in the list. Older ones move to **History**.

<img src="/shots/en/completed.png" alt="The Completed section open under the list, with Call Marta and its Reopen button." width="360" />

## Details, subtasks and a note

Click the title of a priority to open its details. Here you change its level, due time and list, break it into subtasks, and write a note.

<img src="/shots/en/detail.png" alt="The details of Send the proposal. Level High, due today at 13:00, list Today, three subtasks with one done, and the note Use the new price list." width="360" />

## Repeat every day

In the details, set **Repeat** to **Every day**. When you mark the priority done, a fresh copy shows in **Today** the next day.

## Limit of 5 in Today

Turn on **Maximum 5 open priorities in Today** in [Settings](/guide/features/settings). With 5 open in **Today**, you cannot add or move another one there until you finish one.
